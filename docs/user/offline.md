# Offline

After one visit with a network, Yak opens and works with no network at all.
Your data is on the device; the app's files are saved in the browser.

## Works offline

- Timer, entries, projects, reports, notes and tasks.
- Backups to a folder you already allowed.

## Needs a network

- Syncing with your other devices (edits made offline sync later).
- The very first visit on a device.

The header shows **Offline** while the browser has no network.

## Updates

A banner says **A new version is available.** Press **Reload** to switch.
Your data is not touched, and a running timer keeps running.
