# Security policy

## Supported versions

- The latest deployed version at `yak.dynamodal.com`.
- The `main` branch.

## Reporting a vulnerability

- Report privately: GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" (Security tab, private vulnerability reporting), or email `yak@dynamodal.com`.
- Please do not open public issues for vulnerabilities.
- Response is best effort; no time commitments.

## In scope

- The app (`src/`).
- The signaling relay (`worker/`).
- The Obsidian plugin (`obsidian/`).

## Not vulnerabilities

- Trust limits that are documented and accepted are not vulnerabilities; see `docs/sync.md` and `docs/goals.md`.
